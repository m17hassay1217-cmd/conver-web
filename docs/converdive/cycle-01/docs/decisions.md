# Design decisions and proof status

- **Frozen narrative:** splash + 10 sections; numbered Section 01 hero, Sections 02–09, Section 10 contact. Section 10 is a conversion block rather than a dense explanatory chapter.
- **Frozen Section 04 concept:** four proposed problem families; root cause + mediator 2 + mediator 1 + visible problem; first surface yaw exposes thickness; scroll-controlled depth-scaled horizontal coverflow; whole causal chain visible simultaneously.
- **Frozen Section 05:** Diagnose, Prioritize, Engineer, Verify; adaptive instrument right; typography transitions left; final differentiation synthesis transforms the last scene rather than retrieving previous 3 scenes; simple vertical transition to Section 06.
- **Frozen Section 06:** four guided expand/play/collapse workflow demos; changed paths highlighted as proposed intervention; final simplified master enquiry-to-booked-work flow. Do not pretend all four problem families are sequential stages. Account expansion is a branch.
- **Frozen Section 07:** exactly 3 concentric/ascending ripple states—less repetitive work/mental load; more time/capacity/control; possible economics. Opening "What do you actually get?". Smaller to larger macro impact.
- **Frozen Section 08:** Map & Connect, Configure & Test, Launch with Safeguards, Monitor & Maintain. Visual is one persistent customer workflow; layers add readiness/safety, not 1:1 pipeline stages. Final workflow brightens only after stage four.
- **Frozen Section 09:** assumption-based directional calculator, transparent inputs, no guaranteed ROI.
- **Frozen Section 10:** primary voice and text paths, optional WhatsApp, lightweight no-login form. Voice response priority may only be claimed when operationally true.
- **Technical decisions provisional:** exact library versions, production WebGL mesh, hosting, email/booking vendor, payment plan, production copy, final artifacts of image/video generation.
- **Evidence boundary:** sample causal stories and benefits are illustrative; industry claims need validation; no genuine client results, testimonials or proven incremental revenue supplied.
